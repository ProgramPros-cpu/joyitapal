A curated botanical exhibition showcasing the vibrant strokes, serene landscapes, and deeply rooted narratives of village life and natural beauty.

Mountain Serenity

Oil Pastel on Paper Landscape

A vibrant exploration of high-altitude tranquility. This piece captures the crisp mountain air and the striking visual contrast between the lush, rolling green slopes and the fiery autumn trees standing guard by the rustic homestead. The dynamic strokes of pastel breathe life into the stillness of the peaks, inviting the viewer to step into a serene, untamed world.

Morning Routine

Oil Pastel on Paper Rural Life

A tender glimpse into the quiet beauty of rural life. A woman tends to her daily chores, scattering feed for the chickens as the morning sun casts a warm, golden glow over the village courtyard. The detailed textures of the thatched roof and the vibrant pinks of her saree celebrate the harmony and enduring spirit of daily existence.

The Golden Harvest

Oil Pastel on Paper Rural Life

Celebrating the bounty of the earth. The rich yellows and greens highlight the profound connection between nature and the people who nurture it. With careful strokes, Joyita emphasizes the golden hues of the harvested crops resting in the sun, reflecting a deep appreciation for the simple, enduring rhythms of the harvest season.

By the Riverbank

Oil Pastel on Paper Nature Series

Grace and daily rhythm converge by the water's edge. This artwork portrays a woman returning from the gentle river, balancing a traditional terracotta pot. The playful ducks gliding peacefully in the foreground and the vibrant blue sky create a harmonious snapshot of pastoral life, blending human activity seamlessly into the natural landscape.
